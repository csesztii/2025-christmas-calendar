Daily Christmas Wordle (DEMO)

❔How to play?

Since it's a demo site, the word is the same every day.
Countdown to christmas, there is a new Wordle task every dayt to solve.
You can guess as many times as many rows you see. The typed words doesn't have to be existing words.
The spatially correct letters are marked with green colour, the misplaced letters that are needed for the solution are marked with golden colour.
